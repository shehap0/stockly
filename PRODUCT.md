# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner, branch managers, and employees of a small automotive parts business use Stockly during
daily counter and stockroom work. They need to find parts quickly, confirm branch availability,
record sales, and keep inventory accurate. Owners also manage branches, staff, and reporting.

## Product Purpose

Stockly is a shared catalog and operational record for sales and inventory across three branches.
Success means staff can complete common work quickly while the database keeps branch permissions,
stock movements, and sale history consistent.

## Brand Personality

Direct, capable, dependable. The interface should feel calm under busy counter conditions and
make consequential actions and errors easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic analytics dashboards, decorative charts without a task, oversized metric tiles,
excessive gradients, dense nested cards, and controls that hide stock or sale consequences.

## Design Principles

- Put the next operational action close to the information that prompts it.
- Make branch context and stock state visible wherever they affect a decision.
- Keep repeated workflows consistent across catalog, inventory, and sales.
- Communicate saved, pending, unavailable, and failed states plainly.
- Favor readable information density over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, and form labels. Do not rely
on color alone for stock or transaction status. Respect reduced-motion preferences and support
small screens used around the shop.
